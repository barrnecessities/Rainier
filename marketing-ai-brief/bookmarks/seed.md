# Bookmark seed

Paste links and short notes from your X / Twitter bookmarks here, one per line.
The brief script reads this file as a signal of what you care about and expands on
it with fresh research. You don't need full tweet text — a permalink is enough.

How to get links out of X (no native export button):
1. Open https://x.com/i/bookmarks
2. For a tweet you want tracked: ••• menu → "Copy link" → paste below.
3. (Optional) Add a note after the link explaining why it matters.
