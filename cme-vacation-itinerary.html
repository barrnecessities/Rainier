№ 01 · Greece

Crete, quiet.

Mirabello Bay · Sep 25 – Oct 4, 2026

The vibe

The Aegean in October is a particular shade of cobalt — deeper, more serious than the bleached aquamarine of high summer. The InterContinental's terrace hangs above Mirabello Bay on Crete's north-east shoulder, and at this hour the light comes in at a low bronze angle that turns the limestone hills the color of warm bread. The August stampede has dissolved; what remains is the island's actual self — wild thyme on the hillside, a fishing boat crossing the bay at half-throttle, the smell of olive oil and lemon from the hotel kitchen. Warm enough still for swimming, crisp enough now to walk the ruins at Knossos without sweating through your shirt.

№ 02 · France

Paris, autumn.

9th Arrondissement · Oct 9 – 18, 2026

The vibe

Paris in mid-October is the city at its most honest. The tourists have retreated, the chestnuts along the Champs-Élysées have gone amber and rust, and there is a particular quality of morning light in the 9th arrondissement — soft, yellowish, filtering through the zinc mansard rooftops — that makes even a walk to the boulangerie feel significant. The Marriott Opera puts you four minutes from the gilded excess of the Opéra Garnier and ten from the Seine. Afternoons smell like fallen leaves and espresso. The city hums at a frequency that is impossible to replicate.

№ 03 · South Africa

Cape Town, wild.

Gardens · Oct 21 – 30, 2026

The vibe

Cape Town in late October is spring — bold, improbable, and visually overwhelming. Table Mountain changes expression with the cloud: one hour draped in white tablecloth fog, the next brutally clear against a sky so blue it looks post-processed. The Mount Nelson's pink colonial walls sit in the bowl of the mountain's shadow. This is a city that contains multitudes — endemic fynbos perfuming the air, the Indian Ocean meeting the Atlantic, wine estates that rank among Europe's finest. And then, three hours by prop plane, the silence of the Lowveld and a safari at dawn that rearranges your sense of scale entirely.

№ 04 · Japan

Kyoto, koyo.

Higashiyama · Nov 12 – 21, 2026

The vibe

Mid-November is arguably the most beautiful two weeks in all of Japan. The koyo — the annual transformation of the maples into crimson, amber, and gold — peaks across Kyoto's 1,600 temples and shrine gardens between roughly November 15 and 25. Hotel SOWAKA occupies a cluster of restored machiya townhouses on the cobblestones of Higashiyama, the old merchant quarter where geiko still walk at dusk. The morning light on the Philosopher's Path, lined with maples gone impossibly red, rewires your visual memory. Japan's rigorous aesthetic discipline is the counterweight to every fluorescent meeting room of the year before it.

№ 05 · Switzerland

Basel, Glühwein.

The Rhine · Dec 4 – 13, 2026

The vibe

Basel in December is a city that has been preparing for this moment for months. The Christmas market on Barfüsserplatz and Münsterplatz erupts in thousands of warm lights reflected in the Rhine — artisanal wooden stalls, hand-carved ornaments, the mineral tang of Glühwein rising through cold air. The Münster cathedral stands above the river with Romanesque gravity that no Christmas decoration can diminish. Forty-five minutes by train into France, the medieval streets of Colmar are draped in twinkling light and evergreen garland. This is the European winter that people mean when they say they want to feel like they're inside a painting.
